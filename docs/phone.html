Phone rules grab

One-time setup in Safari on your iPhone (about 1 minute). After that, grabbing rules is one tap.

  1. Tap Copy grabber below.
  2. Open any web page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add Bookmark. Name it Grab rules, save.
  3. Open Bookmarks (book icon), tap Edit, tap Grab rules, delete its address, paste the copied text, then Done.

Every campaign

  1. Join the campaign on Content Rewards in Safari (logged in).
  2. Open Bookmarks, tap Grab rules. A purple message says "rules sent". The first time it asks for the helper passcode.
  3. Your phone buzzes when the cloud finishes checking and building.

It only runs when you tap it, on the page you are looking at. It refuses on a campaign you have not joined.
