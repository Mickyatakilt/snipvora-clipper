Rules grabber (bookmark)

One-time setup: drag this button up to your browser's bookmarks bar.

Grab rules

Every time

  1. Open the campaign page on Content Rewards, logged in, after you joined.
  2. Click Grab rules in your bookmarks bar.
  3. Click Send to app in the box. The rules appear on that campaign's card in the dashboard.

It only reads the page you are looking at, in your own browser. It sends only when you click Send. The first time it asks for your helper passcode.
