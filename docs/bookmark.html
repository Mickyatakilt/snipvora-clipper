Rules grabber (bookmark)

One-time setup: drag this button up to your browser's bookmarks bar.

Grab rules

Every time

  1. Open the campaign page on Content Rewards, logged in, after you joined.
  2. Click Grab rules in your bookmarks bar.
  3. A box opens with all the text it found. Click Copy, then paste it to Claude.

It only reads the page you are looking at, in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere until you paste it.
